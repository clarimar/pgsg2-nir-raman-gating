Highlights
- Prior-guided spectral gating was compared with calibrated PLS on NIR and Raman data
- Against cross-validated PLS, the gated model loses in NIR and wins in Raman
- A fixed PLS complexity produced a spurious NIR advantage of +0.23 in R2
- Raman gates are sparser and NIR gates smoother per wavenumber, as physics predicts
- Literature priors make the learned gate reproducible across seeds, not more accurate
